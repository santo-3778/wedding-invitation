Our Story

How It All Began

2018 — The Day We Met

It started with a glance in a college corridor. Then another one on the road. And somehow, even the bus stop became a place worth looking forward to. ❤️

Santo felt the spark instantly—but there was just one problem: he didn't know her name.

What followed was an unofficial college-wide search mission that lasted for months. After looking everywhere, he finally found her… only to discover that the girl he had been searching the entire college for was actually his junior, just one class away. 😀

And that's how a simple glance turned into the beginning of our story.

2019 — The Proposal

A year of conversations, stolen glances, laughter, and countless little moments later, Santo finally decided it was time to stop searching and ask the most important question.

He proposed.

And thankfully, Jolly said yes. ❤️

One year, one question, and one "yes" later, our forever had officially begun.

2027 — The Next Chapter

From a college corridor to countless memories, from a spark at first sight to a love that grew stronger with every passing year—we've come a long way.

Now, after all the searching, finding, and falling in love, we're finally ready for the best chapter yet:

Forever, together.

And we can't wait to celebrate it with the people we love most. 💍❤️
